# Support

Choose the channel that matches the request:

- **Learning, usage, and open-ended questions:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/superflyad/Developer-handbook/discussions)
- **Technical errors or broken examples:** [Content problem issue](https://github.com/superflyad/Developer-handbook/issues/new/choose)
- **New chapter or curriculum proposals:** [Content proposal issue](https://github.com/superflyad/Developer-handbook/issues/new/choose)
- **Website, build, or download failures:** [Site or build issue](https://github.com/superflyad/Developer-handbook/issues/new/choose)
- **Security-sensitive reports:** [Private vulnerability report](https://github.com/superflyad/Developer-handbook/security/advisories/new)

Please do not use security reports for general support or content corrections. Before opening an issue, search existing issues and verify that the problem still appears in the current web edition or `main` branch.

The project does not provide private consulting, production incident response, or support for third-party products described in the handbook.
